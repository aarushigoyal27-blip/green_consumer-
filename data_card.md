## 1. Provenance
- **Origin:** Synthetic, generated by us. Emissions factors come from the UK Government GHG Conversion Factors 2026 (file `emission.csv`).
- **Source(s) and licence:** the customer data has no outside source. The emission factors are a published UK Government dataset; use is limited to its open-government licence terms.
- **Confirmation:** no real personal or confidential banking or customer data is used.

## 2. Generation process
- Generator script: `generate_data.py` · seed: 42 · reproduce with `python generate_data.py --n 2000 --out data`. The same seed gives identical files.
- Each customer gets hidden traits (car dependence, eco-mindedness, digital comfort, energy-use level) plus visible attributes (region, income band, age band, tenure). Everything else follows from these, so patterns are plausible and linked.
- Deliberately encoded: London has fewer cars and more public transport; Scotland and the North use more gas; Northern Ireland rarely has mains gas; owners use more energy; older customers prefer paper statements; fuel spend, mileage, vehicle age and loans drive EV take-up; energy use, home-improvement spend and mortgage drive Green Home; electricity use and outright ownership drive Solar; digital comfort drives Paperless.
- Link to label rules: [label_rules.md](label_rules.md)
- The hidden traits are written to `data/_hidden/` for checking only and are never used as model features.

## 4. Splits
| Split | Share | Unit of split | Rows | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 70% | customer | 1,400 customers | fit models |
| Validation | 15% | customer | 300 customers | compare models, report metrics |
| Test | 15% | customer | 300 customers | scored once at the end |

- **Leakage controls:** the split is made on customers before anything else is built; no customer is in more than one split; features use only data up to 2026-06-30; labels come only from 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31; label-derived and hidden-trait columns are not features. Verified by assertions in `generate_data.py` (no transaction after the cutoff, no take-up outside the outcome window, ineligible pairs have no label, customer IDs unique).

**Not in the file, handled as documented assumptions** (listed in `carbon_engine.py` and echoed into each result): solar yield (900 kWh per kWp per year), solar self-consumption (50%), array size (2–6 kWp), Green Home demand cut (gas 20%, electricity 5%), paper per statement (10 g). Hybrid vehicles have no factor, so their EV impact is reported as insufficient data.

## 7. Assumptions and limitations
- The data is synthetic: patterns are plausible but simpler than real behaviour, and labels follow a known formula plus noise, so model scores overstate real-world performance.
- Merchant names come from a fixed list of templates with small typos, so the categoriser scores near-perfectly; real merchant text is messier.
- The generator has no consent, account status, compliance, arrears, property type or exact age. The app fills demo defaults (consent given, active account, no compliance hold, no arrears, semi-detached house) and uses the age-band midpoint. Real data must supply these.
- Annual energy is a 12-month lookback (July 2025 to June 2026) to avoid seasonal bias; months that are missing are averaged over those present.
